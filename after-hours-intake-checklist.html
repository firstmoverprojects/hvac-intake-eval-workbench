For local service shops whose night and weekend web leads die on the form.

Most shops don’t need another chatbot first. They need to know where the intake path fails after hours — missing callback path, weak urgency capture, no photo prompt, or no human handoff.

Score your public intake path (10 minutes)

  1. Open your live contact / request form on a phone (not just desktop).
  2. Submit a fake after-hours emergency with incomplete address and no photo.
  3. Note what the confirmation says: who calls back, when, and how.
  4. Check whether urgency, trade, service area, and after-hours flag are captured.
  5. Check whether a human can triage the lead without opening five tools.
  6. Ask: if this arrived at 11:40pm Saturday, would revenue be protected?

What “pass” looks like

  • Caller gets a clear next step (call, text, or scheduled callback) — not a dead thank-you.
  • Missing fields are explicit, not silent.
  • Emergency vs quote vs maintenance can be separated without guessing.
  • Someone on-call can act from one screen.
